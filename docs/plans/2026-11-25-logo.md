# Plan: The VNCE logo and banner

**Date:** 2026-11-25 (dark style and banner: 2026-11-26)
**Status:** Approved by the user on 2026-11-26

## Goal

vnce needs a logo, and a banner for the repository's GitHub page, that tell its story in
one picture: from terminal pixels to real pictures. Both follow the dark, minimal style
of the Variation ecosystem's banner for variation-ui (github.com/hanilr/variation-ui):
its horse, seen from the front, starts as big pixels on the left and reaches the smooth
drawing on the right, in front of a farm.

GitHub has no page background; the banner is the repository's **social preview** image
(Settings → Social preview, 1280 x 640), shown when a link to it is shared. The
repository is private for now, so the banner is uploaded by hand once it is public.

Making the logo the default icon of every build is a separate, later phase (Phase 26,
`2026-11-25-phase-26-default-icon.md`).

## Approaches considered

1. **A generator tool (chosen).** The drawing is vector shapes inside a small Rust tool;
   the tool samples it on a grid whose blocks shrink from left to right, and writes the
   SVGs and the PNGs. The transition is exact and everything is regenerated with one
   command.
2. Hand-written SVG including every pixel square: hundreds of squares to align by hand,
   and every change starts over. Dropped.
3. Pixelating with an SVG filter: renders differently between viewers and cannot be
   trusted for PNG and icon output. Dropped.

For the horse, the user chose the variation-ui horse itself (one horse across the
ecosystem) over a new horse drawn in the same style.

## The design

- **Style:** the dark one of the variation-ui banner. Background `#161616`, white
  `#e9e9e9`, and three accents: red `#c83737`, green `#37c837`, blue `#3737c8`.
- **Horse:** the variation-ui horse: white shards around the edge of the head, a red left
  eye, a blue right eye, a green muzzle, and a network of white lines down the nose. The
  shards are traced once from the variation-ui banner into polygons; the nose lines are
  measured by hand and drawn as strokes. The outline of the head is filled with the
  background color so the farm does not show through the face.
- **Farm:** dim grey outlines (`#4a4a4a`): hills, a barn with a cross-braced door, a
  fence and the moon; the banner adds a silo.
- **Transition, left to right:** 32, 16, 8 and 4 px blocks (for the 512 px logo; the
  banner uses the same sizes), then the smooth drawing. Band edges are not straight
  lines: each edge moves by up to one block per row, so the bands interlock.
- **Pixels for line art:** a block takes the color that covers most of it after the
  background, when that color covers at least as much as a line 2.5 px wide crossing the
  block (at most a quarter of the block); otherwise it keeps the background. Shards and
  lines become chunky pixel lines; hairlines grazing a block do not.
- **Logo:** a dark square badge with round corners, the horse centered in it, the farm
  behind it, clipped to the badge; outside the badge is transparent.
- **Banner (1280 x 640):** the horse on the left where the variation-ui banner has it,
  the farm along the bottom, and on the right, in the pixel font of `vnce-raster`
  (5 x 7), centered where the variation-ui banner centers its text:
  - **VNCE**
  - **V**ARIATION **C**LI **E**NGINE (first letters red, green, blue)
  - Make it **PIXEL** (the letters of PIXEL in red, green and blue)
  - Make it SMOOTH ("Make" red and green, "it" blue)
- **Small sizes:** block sizes scale with the output size and never go below one pixel.
  The thin lines get faint at 16 to 64 px; Phase 26 decides what the icon needs.

## How it is built

- `tools/gen-logo/`: a standalone Rust tool (outside the workspace, like
  `tools/gen-block-tables`), depending only on `vnce-raster` (path dependency) for PNG
  encoding and the pixel font. No new crates.
- Modules:
  - `geometry.rs`: paths (move, line, cubic, close), polygons, stroked lines and rings,
    flattening, transforms, SVG path data.
  - `paint.rs`: solid, linear and radial gradient paints (user-space coordinates), their
    evaluation and their SVG definitions, so the SVG and the PNGs use the same math.
  - `horse.rs`: layers, the palette, and the variation-ui horse (traced shards and nose
    lines, in the banner's coordinates).
  - `farm.rs`: the badge and the farm's outlines (barn, silo, fence, curves), and the
    logo's farm.
  - `scene.rs`: a scene: its size, layers, clip shape and band edges; the logo scene.
  - `banner.rs`: the banner scene and its lettering.
  - `text.rs`: pixel text as vector squares.
  - `raster.rs`: anti-aliased rendering of a scene (4 x 4 supersampling, scanline fill
    with the nonzero rule).
  - `pixelate.rs`: the bands, their jittered edges, the block colors.
  - `svg.rs`: the SVG writer (pixel bands as merged `<rect>` runs, the smooth part as
    paths clipped to its region).
  - `main.rs`: `cargo run --release --manifest-path tools/gen-logo/Cargo.toml -- assets/logo`
- Output: `assets/logo/vnce-logo.svg`, `assets/logo/vnce-logo-{16,32,64,256,512}.png`,
  `assets/logo/vnce-banner.svg`, `assets/logo/vnce-banner.png`.
- `README.md` shows the logo at the top. The banner is not in the README; it is uploaded
  as the social preview once the repository is public.

None new (`vnce-raster` from the workspace, by path).

## Risks / open questions

- Taste: the drawing may need rounds of adjustment with the user; regenerating is one
  command.
- The tool is outside the workspace, so CI does not build it; it is checked by hand
  (`cargo test`, `cargo clippy`, `cargo fmt --check` with its manifest).
